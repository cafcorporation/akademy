## Four profiles, one platform

### Cost engineers and estimators
Prepare parametric and detailed CAPEX estimates from **Class 5** (order of magnitude, −50% to +100%) through **Class 2** (control estimate). Component costs, location factors and cost indices are all in one workflow.

### Project managers (PMI / PMBOK)
Start an estimate **in project-management language** without being cost-engineering experts. Kpex translates the project scope into a structured cost breakdown.

### EPC contractors and engineering consultants
Benchmark project costs against historical data, validate feasibility and produce **client-ready PDF reports**.

### Owners, operators and national oil companies (NOCs)
Review the estimates EPCs submit, request equipment pricing through **Kpex Exchange**, and track the full lifecycle cost (**CAPEX → OPEX → ABEX**) in a single platform.
