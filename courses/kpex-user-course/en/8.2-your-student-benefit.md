## Look at what you can already do

You started this course with a question: what makes Kpex unique? Today you can answer it with the tool open in front of you. You now know how to:

- **Classify** an estimate under AACE 18R-97 and explain its PMI mapping.
- **Create** an estimate with the Estimate Wizard and organise it in a WBS.
- **Pick the right tool** for each class: curves and facilities at Class 5–4, components and MTO at Class 3, real quotes at Class 2.
- **Calibrate** any cost for location (LFI) and time (Cost Index), and measure project definition with the PDRI.
- **Cover the whole lifecycle**: CAPEX, OPEX and ABEX.
- **Benchmark and report**: curves, P10/P50/P90 ranges and the three portfolio reports.
- **Request real prices** through Kpex Exchange.

That is exactly what's expected of a professional Kpex user. And we want to recognise it.
