```
Card one, Estimate Identity: the estimate title, the project, the currency and the dates. ## Scene 4 · Cards 3 and 4 · 1:15–1:50
```

- **Speaking:** Carlos · **Layout:** B
- **Background:** `screenshots/4.1-estimate-wizard.png`
- **On-screen text:** *3 · Cost Data (TIC)* · *4 · Basis of Estimate · LFI AUTO · Fetch CAI*

**Script**
```
Card three, Cost Data: the base total installed cost, the TIC, before contingency, plus the contingency and escalation percentages. Card four, Basis of Estimate: you pick the project country and the Location Factor fills itself in, tagged AUTO, though you can override it. For escalation you choose the base year, the execution year and the index, click Fetch CAI, and Kpex works out the percentage.
```

## Scene 5 · Card 5 · Lifecycle scope · 1:50–2:18

- **Speaking:** Carlos · **Layout:** B
- **Background:** `screenshots/5.4-lifecycle-cost-scope.png`
- **On-screen text:** *5 · Lifecycle Cost Scope* · *CAPEX · OPEX · ABEX*

**Script**
```
Card five, Lifecycle Cost Scope: CAPEX is always included. You tick OPEX to add Module D, and ABEX to add Module E. When you do, the plant lifetime appears, used for net present value and for discounting the ABEX. And you finish with Create Estimate.
```

## Scene 6 · The Class 5 extra block · 2:18–2:45

**Script**

```
There's a block that only appears if you choose Class 5: the Cost Curve Basis. You select a cost curve model, enter the plant capacity and the location factor, and click Calculate from Curve. Kpex shows you the low value, the mid-point with contingency and the high value, and auto-fills the TIC on card three. It's the fastest way to start an order-of-magnitude estimate.
```
