## Scene 4 · How an RFQ works · 1:30–2:00

- **On-screen text:** *RFQ in 3 steps* · *1 create · 2 quote · 3 compare* · *replaces parametric → freezes the baseline*

**Script**
```
The heart of the Exchange is the RFQ, the request for quotation. It works in three steps: the owner creates the RFQ describing the equipment package; vendors see it in their portal and submit a quote; and the owner receives all the bids inside Kpex, compares them and keeps the history. In the Class 2 workflow you saw in Module 3, this is the step where you replace parametric costs with real quotes and freeze your control baseline.
```
