## Production notes

- **Pronunciation:** before generating, set in HeyGen's pronunciation dictionary how **Kpex** is said (the way you say it), plus **AACE**, **PDRI** and **CAPEX/OPEX/ABEX**. Generate scene 3 on its own first and listen to it.
- **Numbers are spelt out** ("seven hundred", "one hundred and ninety-six") on purpose, so the avatar reads them correctly. On screen they appear as figures.
- **Pace:** about 360 words ≈ 2:30 at a normal pace. If it runs long, trim scene 7 first.
- **Screenshots** come from a demo account, with no client data. In scene 6 the screenshot shows the estimate currency (USD) but not the units switch: the on-screen text *Metric ⇄ Imperial* covers it. To show the real switch, take a fresh capture with it visible.
- **Export and publish:** export 1080p with captions and upload to YouTube (unlisted) or Vimeo. Then paste the link into `en/1.1-what-makes-kpex-unique.md` as `video_url: https://…` and publish. The video appears at the top of the lesson.
