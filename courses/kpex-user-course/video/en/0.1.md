- **A · Full avatar:** avatar centred or on the left over the dark Akdemy background.

## Production notes

- **Pronunciation:** set how **Kpex** (as you say it), **AACE**, **CAPEX** and **Akdemy** are pronounced in HeyGen's dictionary. Generate scene 3 first (the busiest) and listen to it.
- **Numbers spelled out** ("five hours", "fifteen days", "one point one") on purpose, so the avatar reads them well; on screen they appear as figures.
- **Pace:** ~330 words ≈ 2:20 at normal speed. If it runs long, trim scene 5 first.
- **Screenshots:** from a demo account, no client data.
- **Export and publish:** export at 1080p with captions and upload to YouTube ("unlisted") or Vimeo. Then paste the link into `en/0.1-how-this-course-works.md` as `video_url: https://…` and rebuild. The video appears above the lesson.
