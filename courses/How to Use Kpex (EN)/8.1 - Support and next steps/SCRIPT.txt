If you've made it this far, you've toured the whole platform: the five layers, the estimate with its AACE class, the cost modules, the Z tools, the benchmarks, the reports and the Exchange. All that's left is knowing where to get help when you need it. I'm Carlos Fuenmayor.

There's a channel for every kind of question. For account access, features or billing, write to support. For methodology questions — the alignment of AACE with PMI, or queries about the Location Factor and the Cost Index — write to methodology. And for Enterprise accounts or Data Partner agreements, to partnerships. A practical tip: when you write to methodology, include the AACE class, the project country and the estimate's base month. That's what the technical team needs to reproduce your number.

But before you write to anyone, check the Knowledge Library. It sits in the Kpex sidebar and brings together user guides, AACE reference documents and templates. It's your first stop: very often the answer is already there. Depending on your membership it may show a lock; click it to see which plan unlocks it.

And where is Kpex heading? The main line of development is AI estimators: conversational assistants that take a description of your facility, or questions asked in project-management language, and prepare an order-of-magnitude or feasibility estimate using the same modules you already know, with the Location Factor and Cost Index applied automatically. Everything you've learned in this course is the foundation they run on: understanding what the AI does is what lets you review and defend its result.

Your next steps: save support and methodology to your contacts, open the Knowledge Library and bookmark a guide that will help on your next project, and take the course's final exam. That exam is the gateway to your certificate and your student benefit, which we cover in the next and final lesson. See you there.
