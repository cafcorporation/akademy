The Z six assessments measure what is not cost: how well defined the project is, how productive the labour will be, how ready it is to build, or which location factor to use where no standard one exists. And the CRI, Construction Readiness Index, checks whether the package is ready to mobilise to site.

The one you'll use most is the PDRI, because it links straight to your estimate's AACE class: the better defined the project, the narrower the accuracy range you can declare.
