In the last lesson I said two users can see different dashboards. The reason is the profile type. Kpex adapts its interface to what each user does: estimating a project is not the same job as selling equipment. In this lesson we look at the four profiles, how the sidebar changes with each one, and which one you have. I'm Carlos Fuenmayor.

Kpex adapts its interface to your profile type, and there are four. The Consultant or Estimator, who does cost estimating and sees the whole platform. The Owner or Operator, who reviews contractors' estimates and requests quotations. The Vendor, who lists equipment and services. And the Data Partner, who contributes historical cost data. The sidebar shows each profile only the sections that are useful to it.

Let's see what each one sees. The Consultant or Estimator sees all the groups from the last lesson: Cost Estimation, Cost Modules, Support Tools, Intelligence and Kpex Exchange, plus their Project Portfolio; this is the profile for people who build estimates. The Owner or Operator has a shorter sidebar, focused on two blocks: EPC Cost Estimates, to review the estimates they receive, and Procurement, with My RFQs and New RFQ, to go to the market for prices.

The other two profiles work in their own portal. The Vendor uses the Vendor Portal: managing products with My Products and Add Product, and reviewing open requests in Browse RFQs. The Data Partner works from their portal, contributing historical datasets in My Datasets. Even so, every profile shares two things: the Dashboard and the Kpex Exchange group, the marketplace that connects buyers and vendors.

So which profile do you have? Signing up on your own doesn't ask you: every new account is created as Consultant or Estimator, the profile that sees the whole platform. Your profile type is set during onboarding, not self-service: Owner and Vendor are enabled at signup, and Data Partner is assigned by invitation from CAF Corporation. In My Account, Profile you manage your details, such as your name or avatar, but not the profile type. Your course trial account is a Consultant or Estimator, precisely because that's the one you need for the estimating exercises.

And here's a point that confuses a lot of people: profile and membership are not the same thing. Your profile type decides which sections you see, based on the work you do. Your membership tier decides how much of each section is unlocked: those are the lock icons from the last lesson. Two estimators with the same profile can see different locks if they're on different tiers. Profile decides what you see; membership, how much.

An exercise: compare your sidebar with the table in the lesson and confirm which profile you have; then think about which profile each colleague, client or supplier in your organisation would have.
